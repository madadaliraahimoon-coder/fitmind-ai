BMR & Calorie Calculator

Estimate how many calories your body uses in a day. These are estimates, not exact medical requirements.

Not for everyone: This tool is for healthy adults (18+). It is not suitable for people under 18, pregnant or breastfeeding women, people with medical conditions, or anyone with a history of eating disorders. Please ask a doctor or registered dietitian instead.

How this is calculated

BMR (basal metabolic rate) is the energy your body uses at rest. We use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation:

  • Men: BMR = 10 x weight (kg) + 6.25 x height (cm) - 5 x age + 5
  • Women: BMR = 10 x weight (kg) + 6.25 x height (cm) - 5 x age - 161

Maintenance calories = BMR x an activity multiplier (1.2, 1.375, 1.55, 1.725 or 1.9).

Real needs vary by person because of muscle mass, genetics, health, and daily movement. Treat the result as a starting point and watch how your body responds over several weeks.


Medical disclaimer: General information only, not medical advice. Do not use calorie estimates to justify extreme dieting. If you struggle with food or body image, please talk to a healthcare professional.
