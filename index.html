Beginner Workout (No Equipment)

Do this 3 days a week with a rest day between. Warm up for 5 minutes first.

  • Bodyweight squats: 2 sets of 10
  • Wall or knee push-ups: 2 sets of 8
  • Glute bridges: 2 sets of 12
  • Plank: 2 holds of 20 seconds

Rest 60 seconds between sets. Stop if you feel sharp pain.
