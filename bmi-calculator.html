BMI Calculator (Adults)

Body Mass Index (BMI) compares your weight with your height. It is a quick screening number, not a diagnosis.

Adult BMI Categories

BMICategory
Below 18.5Underweight
18.5 to 24.9Healthy weight range
25.0 to 29.9Overweight
30.0 and aboveObesity range

Limitations of BMI

  • BMI does not measure body fat. Muscular people may have a high BMI without excess fat.
  • It does not show where fat is stored, and it can be less accurate for older adults and some ethnic groups.
  • These categories are for adults only. Children and teens need age-and-sex-specific growth charts from a doctor.
  • BMI is not a diagnosis. Talk to a healthcare professional about your health.
