Answer a few questions and get a simple weekly plan. This planner follows built-in rules in your browser. It is not an AI and it does not replace a coach or doctor.

Your Weekly Plan

Warm-up (5 minutes, every session)

  • March in place: 1 minute
  • Arm circles and shoulder rolls: 1 minute
  • Hip circles and gentle torso twists: 1 minute
  • Slow bodyweight squats: 10 reps
  • Easy leg swings, 10 each side (hold a wall for balance)

Cool-down (5 minutes, every session)

  • Walk slowly for 2 minutes until your breathing settles
  • Stretch calves, thighs, hamstrings, chest and back: hold each 20 to 30 seconds
  • Stretch gently, with no bouncing and no sharp pain

Progress safely

  • Keep a log. When you can do the top of the rep range on every set with good form, make it slightly harder (a tougher variation, a bit more weight, or one more set).
  • Increase only one thing at a time and keep changes small.
  • Rest days matter: walk, stretch, and sleep well.

Safety instructions

Stop right away and seek medical help if you feel chest pain, dizziness, unusual shortness of breath, or sharp pain. Use good form before adding difficulty. Warm up first. Stay hydrated. If an exercise hurts, use the alternative listed or skip it. This plan is general information, not medical advice.
